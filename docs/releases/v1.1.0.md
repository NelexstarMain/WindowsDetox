Windows Detox 1.1.0

Unsigned installer: Windows SmartScreen may warn (More info, Run anyway). Try it in a VM first.

## New
- Calendar plugin 1.1.0: a Google Calendar-style Day, Week and Month planner. Drag on the grid to create a block, then move, resize, multi-select (Ctrl+click), duplicate or delete it. Recurring series are supported, and focus sessions start by themselves at their time.
- New event blocks: solid, rounded and colour-coded by kind. Past blocks are muted, and the selected block has a ring.
- New quick editor: an "Add title" field, Focus session and Task chips, the date and time on one line, a pinned Save button and "More options".
- Allowlist: apps are shown as a grid of large icons. Click a tile to allow the app; the path and badges are in the tooltip, and running apps carry a dot.

## Improved
- Goals & Tasks: a visible delete icon on each task, toggles aligned at the right edge, a compact −/+ daily goal, and rows that no longer jump when you tick a task.
- Settings: compact −/+ steppers, and confirmations that say what they confirm ("Clear finished entries? Click again").
- Plugin pages share the app's page header and spacing. The dashboard widgets fit their content better, with clearer contrast in Focus Insights and on the Plugins page.
- Plugin SDK (additive): `UiTimeGrid` for schedule views, compact and icon-only buttons, field styles (title, compact, chips), read-only note fields, a form footer link, and the `Edit`, `Copy` and `Delete` icons.

## Fixed
- Clicking a page no longer scrolls it by about 30 px or cuts off the title. A calendar drag now creates exactly the dragged time range.
- Mouse clicks no longer draw a keyboard focus rectangle around the page content.
- The calendar's Day/Week/Month buttons no longer shift as the date label changes.
- Data storage hardening: a default service DACL for swept folders, HMAC key retry and quarantine of damaged files, atomic-write temp cleanup, journal version checks, byte-accurate plugin storage quotas, and error messages without paths.
